🚫 Deliberately not compared

Per-environment identifiers and Power BI runtime state. They are preserved in Target on every write.
🎯
Identity matches the engine, not the file. Objects are paired case-insensitively, the way Analysis Services treats names, and per-environment GUIDs in partition names are normalised, so a model compared against itself reports no differences instead of a wall of false Added/Removed pairs.
